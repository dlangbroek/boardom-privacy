# Boardom Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 1, 2026**

Boardom ("the app") is a board game rules reference built by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control or delete it.

## Information You Provide

**Account.** Creating an account requires an email address and password, handled by our backend provider, Supabase. Your email is used only for sign-in and account-related emails (like password resets) — it is never shown to other players.

**Profile.** When you set up a profile you choose a display name, a color, and optionally upload a photo as your avatar. Your name, color, and avatar are visible to other players — for example, next to your game ratings or in a group you've joined.

**Game activity.** Ratings, wishlist, favorites, and downloaded-games lists are tied to your account so they sync across your devices. Ratings are shown publicly (as part of each game's community rating); your wishlist, favorites, and downloads are private to you.

**Social features.** If you use Friends, Groups, Game Nights, or Tournaments, the names of people you invite or connect with, group membership, and game night/tournament results are stored so the feature works. House rules you write are shown according to the visibility setting you choose (everyone, friends, or just you).

## Camera

The camera is used only to scan a game's barcode or a card, so the app can look up which game it belongs to. Boardom does not save or upload photos. When you scan a box barcode, the barcode number itself is sent to a third-party lookup service (upcitemdb.com) to identify the product — no other information is sent with it.

## Advertising

Boardom shows ads through Google AdMob to support development. On iOS, the app asks for your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before using data for personalized ads — you can decline this and the app works exactly the same either way, just with less-targeted ads. See Google's privacy policy for how AdMob handles data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## What's Stored on Your Device

Some information — your language preference, accessibility settings, theme, and a cached copy of the game library and your social data — is stored locally on your device (via standard app storage) so the app works offline and loads quickly. This local data is removed if you delete the app.

## Third Parties We Use

- **Supabase** — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage (profile avatars).
- **Google AdMob** — serves ads and, if you allow tracking, personalizes them.
- **upcitemdb.com** — looks up a product name from a scanned barcode.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone beyond what's needed to run the features above.

## Your Rights

You can edit or delete your profile information, ratings, wishlist, favorites, and house rules at any time from within the app. You can permanently delete your account — along with your profile, ratings, wishlist, favorites, groups you own, and everything else tied to your account — from the Profile tab ("Delete Account"). This action can't be undone.

## Children's Privacy

Boardom is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
